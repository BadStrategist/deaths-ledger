OSRS Hardcore Worlds

Launch time, death rules and everything Death Toll tracks. One life per character, so read this first.

Launch

Hardcore Worlds launch on Wednesday 4 November 2026 at 11:00 UTC.

Every character gets one life. Die an unsafe death and that life is over: start the character again, or move it to the main game.

The death rules

Hardcore Worlds use the Hardcore Group Ironman death ruleset. An unsafe death happens in two stages:

  1. You lose almost everything you carried. You keep your 3 most valuable items, or 4 with Protect Item. Skulled, you keep 0, or 1 with Protect Item. Anything that would normally go to your gravestone is deleted.
  2. You choose: Reset or Transfer. Reset wipes the character, kept items included, and you start again on Hardcore Worlds. Transfer moves the character to the main game for good, with the items you kept, your bank and your other storage.

Some deaths don't count. Castle Wars, Clan Wars, Soul Wars, Emir's Arena and the TzHaar Fight Pit are safe. Nightmare Zone, Pest Control, the TzHaar Fight Cave, the Inferno and your own house are not. Look up the rest on Is it safe?

Questions

When do OSRS Hardcore Worlds launch?

Wednesday 4 November 2026 at 11:00 UTC.

What happens when you die on Hardcore Worlds?

Each character has one life. After an unsafe death you keep your 3 most valuable items (4 with Protect Item) and anything that would go to your gravestone is deleted. Then you choose: Reset and start the character over on Hardcore Worlds, or Transfer it to the main game for good.

Which deaths are safe?

Castle Wars, Clan Wars, Soul Wars, Emir's Arena and the TzHaar Fight Pit are safe. A few are safe only in part, like Koschei's 4th phase in The Fremennik Trials and replays of Galvek and Glough. Most other deaths end your run, including the Nightmare Zone, Pest Control, the Chambers of Xeric and the TzHaar Fight Cave. Search any of them on Is it safe?

What items are kept on death?

Your 3 most valuable items, or 4 with Protect Item. Skulled, you keep 0, or 1 with Protect Item. Everything else that would go to your gravestone is deleted. Build your loadout on Kept on death to see exactly what you'd lose.

How do I track a player?

Enter their RuneScape name on Track a player. You'll see their total level, skills and bosses while they live, and if they fall, their grave goes up in the Graveyard.

What are world firsts?

The first players on Hardcore Worlds to reach each milestone: first to 2,000 total, first to max, the first 99 in every skill and the first name on every boss and raid. Follow the race on World firsts.
