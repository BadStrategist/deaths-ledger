World firsts

The first players on Hardcore Worlds to reach each milestone, with one life to do it in. A skull means they got there, then fell.

NOT OPEN YETHardcore Worlds opens 4 November. First claims appear here within minutes of the hiscores going live.

First spotted on the official hiscores, checked every ~10 minutes. Boss and raid tables only list players after the hiscores' minimum kill count.
