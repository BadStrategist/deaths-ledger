Kept on death

Build your loadout and see which items you keep and which you lose for good if you die on Hardcore Worlds.

The Hardcore Worlds rule

  • Gravestone items are deleted. Anything that would normally go to your gravestone is permanently deleted.
  • You keep your 3 most valuable items, or 4 with Protect Item. Skulled, you keep 0, or 1 with Protect Item.
  • After dying you choose Reset or Transfer:
    • Reset wipes the character, including the items you kept. You start again on Hardcore Worlds.
    • Transfer moves the character to the main game for good, along with the items you kept, your bank and your other storage.

Source: Jagex, Hardcore Worlds are coming on November 4th. Kept-item rules: OSRS Wiki, Items Kept on Death. This is a pre-launch calculator. Always check the in-game Items Kept on Death screen before you take a risk.

            What this doesn't cover

            • Ranking uses live prices. The game ranks items by its own value, which is usually close to the Grand Exchange guide price. These are live trade prices, so items with close values can swap places. Treat near-ties as unsure.
            • Stacks count one item at a time. If a stack is among your most valuable items you keep that many units of it (for example 3 death runes), not the whole stack.
            • Untradeable items are not listed. Some are always kept, some turn into coins or components, and some are lost. Those rules are item by item, so this calculator doesn't guess. Check the wiki page and the in-game screen.
            • Not modelled: PvP deaths (where the killer gets your loot), safe minigames, instanced bosses, and items with their own death rules (looting bag contents, rune pouch, Trouver parchment, and so on).
