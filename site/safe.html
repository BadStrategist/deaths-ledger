How a Hardcore death works

Hardcore Worlds use the Hardcore Group Ironman death ruleset. An unsafe death happens in two stages:

  1. You lose almost everything you carried. You keep your 3 most valuable items (4 with Protect Item). Anything that would normally go to your gravestone is deleted.
  2. You choose: Reset or Transfer. Reset starts the character over. Transfer moves the character off Hardcore Worlds for good.

Source: Jagex, "Hardcore Worlds are coming on November 4th"

Escape crystals

An escape crystal costs 10,000 gp. Once set up, it activates on its own after a period of inactivity and teleports you away.

Source: Jagex, "Hardcore Worlds are coming on November 4th"
